# 🚀 Products & Platforms

### 🏢 VersalFlow CRM

Enterprise CRM with lead management, sales pipelines, customer support, WhatsApp integration, call tracking, automation, and analytics.

### 🛒 E-Commerce Platform

Scalable commerce ecosystem supporting products, inventory, orders, payments, shipping, and customer management.

### 📱 E-Commerce Mobile Application

Android and iOS application for seamless shopping, order tracking, customer engagement, and promotions.

### 📦 ODMS – Order & Dealer Management System

Dealer onboarding, order processing, stock visibility, distribution management, and operational reporting.

### 👥 Visitor Management System

Digital visitor registration, approvals, visitor tracking, security logs, and reporting.

### ✅ Task Management Platform

Team collaboration, project tracking, task assignments, workflow automation, and productivity analytics.

### 🌿 Flora Application

Business-focused digital platform designed to streamline operations, customer engagement, and workflow management.

### 💬 WhatsApp Business Platform

Customer communication, broadcasting, support ticketing, automation, templates, and campaign management.

### ☎️ IVR & Call Management System

Call routing, agent assignment, recordings, analytics, call tracking, and customer support workflows.

### 🤖 AI Business Automation Suite

AI-powered lead qualification, smart recommendations, business insights, workflow automation, and reporting.

### 📊 Business Analytics Platform

Real-time dashboards, sales tracking, customer insights, operational metrics, and performance monitoring.

### 📋 Inventory Management System

Stock control, warehouse management, purchase tracking, supplier management, and inventory forecasting.

### 🎯 Customer Support Platform

Multi-channel support system integrating calls, WhatsApp, email, tickets, and customer history.

### 🌐 Web Applications

Custom business applications built using modern technologies for startups, enterprises, and growing businesses.

### 📲 Mobile Applications

Cross-platform mobile applications focused on performance, scalability, and user experience.
